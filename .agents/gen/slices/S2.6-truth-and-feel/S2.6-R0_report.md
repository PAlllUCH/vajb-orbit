## 2. Blocking findings (HIGH) — the wave cannot land as written

  outward radial (**rock centre → spawn point**)"), and the brief's pin block

- **Tree:** `Asteroid.eject_velocity()` (`game/asteroid.gd:291-292`) takes **no argument** and

  velocity `:364-371`. The outward radial is *exactly* that same placement direction, so the
  kick belongs on `:363-371`, next to the RNG (`rng`, `:104`, seeded by `setup()` `:122-129`).
- **Consequence:** R2's set is `vajb-orbit/game/asteroid.gd,vajb-orbit/tests/` (brief

- **Cure, owner's call:** extend R2's `VAJB_WORKER_FILES` with

### F6 — `COAST_TIME_MULT 2.0` and the owner's slide complaint pull in opposite directions
- The pin reverts the drag retune to give the ship its carry back (§14:1236-1240), while the same
  bullet demands the slide be symmetric and small. In this model the lateral decay *is* the drag:
  `_linear_damp() = 1 / coast_time` (`game/player_ship.gd:869-872`) is handed to the body
  (`:891`), `_thrust_axis` compensates damp **only along the commanded axis** (`:706-714`), and
  `_step_strafe` early-returns with no lateral command (`:695-698`). So the velocity's component
  across the nose decays at `1 / coast_time` — **halving the damp doubles that time constant**,
  which is exactly the "slides in one side" read (the hull tracks to the outside of a turn).
- **What R0 could not find:** no left/right asymmetry exists in the force/torque path. I checked
  every coupling site — `_step_turn` is pure `apply_torque` (`:652-661`), `_thrust_axis` is
  `apply_central_force` on the axis (`:714`), `_command_velocity` caps the stick at unit length
  (`:672-678`), `_strafe_axis` = heading + π/2 (`:684-685`), `_manual_strafe` = right − left
  (`:552-557`), `_turn_toward`/`_aim_turn` are `wrapf`-symmetric (`:607-610`, `:624-629`). The
  candidate single-sided mechanisms are (i) the outward drift above, whose magnitude scales with
  `coast_time`, and (ii) the cursor's W gate (F2). R4 must measure the sign and the magnitude per
  class before choosing the seam fix — the brief already demands this; flagging that AC5 and AC6
  are in tension is the part the pin does not say.
- **Disclosure R4 owes (already true for the retuned column, §4:135-137):** the column reaches
  every NPC hull through `ShipStats`, so `ACCEL_TIME_MULT` and `COAST_TIME_MULT` also halve every
  NPC's acceleration and double every NPC's carry/damp. No NPC file needs editing, but
  `test_engine2_npc.gd` and the NPC probes are where that would show up.
