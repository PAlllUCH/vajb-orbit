Rules:
1. **The forces are the class's; the inertia is the fit's.** `ShipStats` gains
   `base_mass` (the handling row's `hull_mass`, read **before** the armour pass) and the
   derived `engine_thrust` (`base_mass × max_speed / accel_time`, the resolved rates).
   The four force sites (`_thrust_axis`, `_step_lateral_drag`, `_step_release`'s brake,
   `_step_turn`'s torque via a class-mass `_angular_inertia()` twin) swap the mass factor
   to `base_mass`; `_body.mass`/`_body.inertia` keep the fitted mass. Nothing else in the
   flight law moves: no `max_speed`/`BRAKE_MULT`/`ANGULAR_DAMP_MULT`/multiplier change.
2. **Plating pays once, in mass.** `_apply_speed`'s armour branch multiplies
   `stats.hull_mass` by `1 + |penalty|` (beside any `mass_add`) and the three
   `stats.*_time *= mass` lines are **deleted** (the Lightened adjustment and the
   max_speed cut stay exactly as shipped). `h_composite` therefore resolves
   ×1.10 (penalty) × 1.10 (`mass_add`) = ×1.21 — ~9 % ponderouser than today's double
   read; accepted, disclosed in §14's bullet, reversal below.
3. **Fuel is thrust's receipt, not a class column.** The boost burn is
   `BOOST_FUEL_REF × engine_thrust / engine_thrust_vanguard` with `BOOST_FUEL_REF := 3.0`
   (the Vanguard anchors it exactly) — §13's eight rates. The dash spends
   `DASH_FUEL_REF(25) × base_mass / 110`. `fuel_max` stays the flat 200 (M5 kept flat).
   NPCs burn no fuel and carry no fit — no NPC number moves (disclose, do not edit).
4. **The rock's mass follows its size.** `ROCK_MASS_DENSITY := 560.0 / (42.0 × 42.0)`
   (the M class anchors it), `_configure_body()` sets `mass = ROCK_MASS_DENSITY ×
   _radius × _radius`, and **`_configure_body()` moves after `_build_look()` in
   `setup`** so the built `_radius` exists — the reorder is RNG-neutral and must be
   disclosed as a deviation. `ROCK_MASS_MULT`/`ROCK_MASS_REFERENCE` stay as the
   SUPERSEDED record with no live reader (the `FRAGMENT_SPLIT` precedent).
5. **Rocks meet rocks and nothing more.** `COLLISION_MASK := 3`; no contact monitor is
   added to `asteroid.gd` (R2: a nudge, not a fight); `can_sleep` stays false.
6. **Placement never interpenetrates.** A minimum-separation pass (margin
   `PLACEMENT_MARGIN := 8.0` u, beside the ring constants) guards both the field spawn
   (`_rock_position` against the already-placed rocks, bounded retries, the field's
   seeded RNG only) and `_deploy_debris` (siblings separated along their ring). After
   any spawn and any cleave: centre distance ≥ r_i + r_j + margin.
7. **The split speed is random and mass-weighted.** `_deploy_debris` scales the child's
   whole ejection vector (inherited ×1.2 part + the outward kick) by a per-child jitter
   uniform `(FRAGMENT_SPEED_JITTER.x, .y) = (0.7, 1.3)` times
   `pow(m_M / child_mass, FRAGMENT_MASS_SPEED_EXP)` with the exponent **0.5** and
   `m_M` the M-class mass. Both constants live beside `FRAGMENT_EJECT_*`.
8. **Nothing else moves**: the S22.5 ore constants, `split_mix`, the kick 150, the cone
   360, both rock damps (0.35/0.25), `damage.gd`, the collision maths, the mining
   arithmetic. A fix that seems to need one is a bucket-2 report.
9. **No new numbers**: every constant this wave writes is §13's rows and §3.2's law.
   If a value is missing, report it and leave it.

## 5. Spec extract (verbatim, cited)

> **The mass law (S22.7, owner 2026-09-30): the forces are the class's; the inertia is
> the fit's.** `thrust = class_mass × max_speed / accel_time` and the coast brake
> `class_mass × max_speed / coast_time` are derived once from the handling row's own
> `hull_mass` (`ShipStats` gains `base_mass`; no new literal), and the thrust/brake/
> turn-torque laws apply them against the **fitted** mass — so `mass_add` plating
> finally makes a hull slower and a heavier fit coasts further and turns ponderouser.

> **Rock mass** — `mass = ROCK_MASS_DENSITY × r²` with the density anchored so the M
> class keeps today's 560 t: S 183 · M 560 · L 1 383 · XL 2 571 t (radii 24/42/66/90 u).
> Reversal: flat `ROCK_MASS_MULT 4.0 × ship_miner` (560 t every class).
> **Rock-rock contact** — `COLLISION_MASK` 2 → 3: rocks meet each other as well as the
> hull. A rock-rock impact is a **nudge, not a fight** — rocks carry no contact monitor.
> Placement gains a minimum-separation pass (margin 8 u). Reversal: mask 2, and the
> separation pass reverts to the pure ring rolls.

`18_engine_spec.md` §13's ejection and fuel rows (amended, binding):
> **Ejection** — `current_velocity × 1.2`, uniform random direction, times a per-child
> speed jitter uniform (0.7, 1.3) and the mass weighting `(m_M / m_child)^0.5`. Reversal:
> jitter (1.0, 1.0) and exponent 0.0.
> **`BOOST_FUEL`** — burn ∝ thrust: `burn = thrust / Isp`, `Isp` anchored so the
> Vanguard's burn stays 3.0/s → fighter 2.75 · corvette 3.10 · miner 1.81 · trader 3.12 ·
> gunship 2.38 · patrol 3.22 · freighter 1.94 · destroyer 2.26 per second. Reversal: flat
> 3.0/s. **`DASH_FUEL`** — `25 × mass / 110 t`: fighter 18.2, destroyer 68.2.
> Reversal: flat 25.

`CONTRACTS.md` §23.5 (unchanged, binding):
> released from a commanded forward+strafe at cruise, the hull's velocity **direction
> holds within 5°** of its release bearing while the speed falls to 0.1× of release —
> the two components decay together, one stop, one line.
