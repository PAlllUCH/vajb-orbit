Rules:
1. **The multiplier divides the gun door only** (§5.3 A5): `apply_gun_work`'s work
  passes through `size_toughness_mult[size_class()] × toughness`; the **mining**
  door (`apply_work`, the mining laser's cycle) is untouched, so the pinned
  1.2 s/unit pace and every mining payout row stand.
2. **The roll is per rock and kept for life** (§5.3 A1): rolled at spawn on the
  **field's own seeded RNG** (the same generator the size/tier rolls use), stored
  on the rock, and readable through an accessor so a suite can assert it without
  guessing.
3. **A rock with no ore cracks on a budget, not on the first point** (§5.3 A3):
  `fragment_work[class]` is that budget. Nothing else about a gun-born child
  changes — `bore 0` (pays nothing) and "never splits further" (§5.2 ter) both
  stand.
4. **A splinter is a real body, not a particle** (§5.3 A4): an S-class
  `AsteroidField` child, `bore 0`, the S budget, ejected on the field's existing
  cone + outward-kick shape, and it never splits further.
5. **No new numbers**: every constant this wave writes is a §5.3 row. If a value
  is missing, report it and leave it — do not invent one.
6. **The economics do not move**: Rule A (no method mints ore), the split table,
  the spawn mix, `tier_base_yield`, `yield_variance_*`, `GUN_BURST_SHARE` and the
  reservoir arithmetic are read-only this wave.

## 5. Spec extract (verbatim, cited)
`02_minerals.md` §5.3, the operative rows:
> **A1** Per-rock toughness roll — every rock rolls `toughness` uniform **0.80–1.60** at spawn on the field's own seeded RNG and keeps it for life.
> **A2** Size carries toughness (`SIZE_TOUGHNESS_MULT`), applied as a **divisor on the gun door only**: **S 1.5 · M 2.5 · L 4.0 · XL 6.0**.
> **A3** Debris gets a real budget (`FRAGMENT_WORK`, work units by the child's own class): **S 2.0 · M 3.0 · L 4.5**.
> **A4** Chip splinters off large rocks: a gun hit that does **not** crack an L or XL rock rolls **25 %** to shed one splinter, at most **1 per 0.5 s** per rock.
> **A5** Mining keeps today's pace: A2 divides the gun door only.

`02_minerals.md` §5.2 ter (unchanged, binding):
> "parentage, not ore, gates a *fragment's* cleave. Every rock born of a cleave splits again **per its own size class** whatever its bore"; "a 0-bore fragment pays nothing at any shatter".

`01_economy_core.md` §5.6:
> shooting a rock never out-earns mining — a gun-attributed shatter pays at most `GUN_BURST_SHARE × _bore_ore`.

## 6. Acceptance list (numbered; the report answers each with a cite)
- **A1** — every spawned rock carries a toughness in **[0.80, 1.60]**, rolled on the
  field's seeded RNG, readable off the rock; two fields with the same seed roll the
  same values; two runs of one field roll different ones.
- **A2** — a gun hit's work divides by `size_toughness_mult[class] × toughness`;
  measured time-to-crack for a T1 rock at the mean roll is **S ≥ 3.0 s · M ≥ 5.0 s ·
  L ≥ 8.0 s · XL ≥ 12.0 s** of `w_laser` 30 dps (state the work totals too), so S
  and XL no longer behave alike.
- **A3** — mining is untouched: the mining laser still realises 1.2 s per ore unit,
  a mining shatter's payout and reserve rows are unchanged, and no mining row in
  `test_s13_*` moves.
- **A4** — a gun-born fragment needs `FRAGMENT_WORK[class]` chip work to crack:
  a single 10-damage chip (work 1.0) no longer cracks an S fragment, a ram does not
  insta-crack one, and an S fragment survives ≥ 0.6 s of `w_laser`.
- **A5** — a gun-born fragment still has `bore 0` (pays nothing at any shatter) and
  still never splits further; `cleaves()`'s parentage rule is intact.
- **A6** — an L/XL rock under sustained fire sheds splinters at 25 % per
  non-cracking hit, **capped at 1 per 0.5 s per rock**; each splinter is a real
  S-class body with `bore 0` and the S budget, ejected outward; S/M rocks shed none;
  a cracking hit spawns the normal split set and no splinter.
- **A7** — no ore is minted: a fully shot family realises ≤ `GUN_BURST_SHARE ×
  _bore_ore` (a before/after measurement of the same seeded fixture), the split
  table and spawn mix are byte-identical, and the sealed files are untouched.
