# Vajb Orbit — Space Environment Art Research

Date: 2026-09-16 · Scope: free CC0 space environment art for a 2D top-down Dark Orbit clone (Godot 4.7.2, Forward+).
Style baseline: Kenney flat vector (Space Shooter Redux/Remastered).
Method: every candidate page below was opened and its license badge/text read directly. Nothing was downloaded.
Policy reference: `docs/ASSETS.md` (CC0-only; CC-BY is an exception, needs an exact credit string).

## Summary

- **Nothing is missing license-wise.** CC0 covers all five needs: tileable nebula/starfield, planets, stations, extra asteroids, sector-map art. No CC-BY is required for anything, so the strict rule costs us nothing here.
- **Two packs carry most of the value:** Screaming Brain Studios' *Seamless Space Backgrounds* (32 seamless 512/1024 nebulae + starfields) and the same author's *2D Planet Pack 1/2* (96 and 420 rendered planets with alpha). Both are CC0 with the badge confirmed on the page.
- **Kenney has more than we already own:** *Planets* (50 files, 2D, CC0) and *Simple Space* (48 files, 2D, tagged `space`, `sci-fi`, `map`) were not in the starter inventory. *Simple Space* is the closest thing found to dedicated sector-map art.
- **The one real gap is style, not license.** No CC0 flat-vector (Kenney-look) top-down space station/docking sprite exists. Available CC0 stations are pixel art (ChaosShark, 64×64 jam pieces) and read as a different game. Recommendation in §Recommendations: render stations from Kenney's CC0 3D space kits with a top-down orthographic camera, which keeps the whole project on Kenney art.
- **Sector map background** is best composed rather than sourced: a seamless nebula tile (SBS) + a transparent star layer (Bonsaiheldin) + Kenney *Planets* nodes.

## Recommendations

1. **Primary download batch (all CC0, no attribution owed).** In priority order:
   1. Kenney *Planets* — https://kenney.nl/assets/planets
   2. Kenney *Simple Space* — https://kenney.nl/assets/simple-space (inspect on a contact sheet first; if it is not star-map art, drop it)
   3. Screaming Brain Studios *Seamless Space Backgrounds* (1024 zip)
   4. Bonsaiheldin *Stars — parallax backgrounds*
   5. Screaming Brain Studios *2D Planet Pack 2* medium tier (512/256/128)
2. **Backdrop architecture in Godot.** Compose instead of hunting a single "sector background": one seamless nebula tile (SBS) as a `Sprite2D` with `texture_repeat` + `region_enabled`, one Bonsaiheldin transparent star layer at a slower `ParallaxBackground` factor, and Kenney planets/stations as regular gameplay nodes. This keeps VRAM low and lets sector colour change by swapping one 1024² tile.
3. **Stations — render them, do not source them.** No CC0 flat-vector top-down station exists (verified across OGA, Kenney, itch listings, Openverse). Kenney's *Modular Space Kit* and *Space Station Kit* (3D, CC0) can be rendered top-down orthographically to 2D sprites, which keeps 100% Kenney provenance and a single art style. Fallback: ChaosShark's CC0 station hulls as placeholders until that pipeline exists.
4. **Asteroids.** Adopt *Asteroid Tileset 01* for destructible tiers (large → medium → small, 8 rotations each) and keep the Kenney meteors for decorative scatter. The two are both flat-ish rocks and can coexist after matching brightness.
5. **Record provenance anyway.** CC0 needs no credits, but run `create_asset_manifest` + `generate_credits` after each batch so `docs/ASSETS.md`'s inventory table stays truthful, and note the exact source page per pack.
6. **Watch these two things on download:** (a) Kenney zips are unversioned links that change with releases, so archive the zip, not just the extracted folder; (b) the larger SBS tiers are 26 MB for the 1024 zip and 7.9 MB for the 512 zip — take the medium/small tier unless 512 px planets are genuinely needed.
