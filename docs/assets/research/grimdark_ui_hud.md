# Grimdark UI / HUD Art Scouting — CC0 Candidates

Research date: 2026-09-16. Scope: black / dark-metal panels and frames, gothic sci-fi ornamentation, industrial gauges and consoles, weathered bars and meters, dark heavy-or-brutalist sci-fi fonts, damage-warning overlays.
Constraint: **CC0 only** (`docs/ASSETS.md`). GPL, CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, OGA-BY and unclear entries are rejected even when visually strong.
Prior pass: `docs/assets/research/ui_hud.md` (flat-vector baseline survey). This report **does not repeat its entries**; it only references them where a grimdark build must sit on top of that baseline. **Nothing was downloaded.**
Method: `assetmcp` search tools (`search_opengameart`, `search_kenney_assets`, `search_openverse_images`, `search_assets`) plus OpenGameArt advanced search with the **CC0 facet `field_art_licenses_tid[]=4`**, tag facets (`ui`, `dark`, `industrial`, `sci-fi`), the OGA collections *Menus and Huds (non pixel art)* and *CC0 Fonts*, and the GGBotNet author listing. Every candidate row below was confirmed by **reading the asset page itself** (OGA `License(s)` badge rendered from `license_images/cc0.png`, plus any attribution notice). itch.io was **not** opened — no page was pre-confirmed to state CC0 in writing, and `search_assets` returned only `blocked_unclear_license` itch rows.

## Summary

- **There is no CC0 grimdark UI family.** Two full passes (this one and `ui_hud.md`) found no CC0 pack that is grimdark as authored. Grimdark has to be a **treatment layer** applied to flat CC0 art: darken/desaturate, blend a rust or panel texture at low alpha, add scratch/dirt/blood decals, retint. Every asset below is chosen for how well it survives that treatment, not for being dark already.
- **The dark-art sources that do exist are small, ancient, and element-based**, not families: `UI Elements` (kindland, 2014), `SCIFI UI` (AR, 2016, explicitly tagged `dark`), `Old Unused UI Stuff` (StumpyStrust, 2018), and Wenrexa's `Assets Free Interface UI Dark Miko`. All are CC0 and all carry PSD or pre-rasterised elements, so they compose rather than tile.
- **Fonts are the strongest result of this pass.** `GGBotNet Fonts CC0 (All-in-1)` ships **45 true vector fonts (TTF/OTF/WOFF/WOFF2)** under CC0 and is the single most valuable find; within it sits `Blaec`, a **CC0 blackletter** (tags `bold`, `black`, `blackletter`, `vector`, 836 glyphs, 90 languages) which is the only genuinely brutalist CC0 display face located. `Boxy Bold - TrueType Font` (CC0) is the chunky block alternative, and `bold boxy fat font` (CC0, bitmap, "Cool Metal" gradient fill) reads as old DOS industrial signage. No CC0 heavy condensed slab or military-stencil TTF exists — that gap remains open.
- **Damage-warning overlays are the best-covered grimdark need.** Four CC0 sets: `Health Overlay Images` (1920×1080, 8.5 MB), `Bloody Background` (1920×1080, authored to fade in as health nears zero via a sin-driven alpha), `Blood overlay` (1280×800, stretchable, commenters explicitly call it a "damage flash"), and `Blood Splatter` (high-res). `Cooldown overlay` (24 slices, 50 % transparent) covers ability-readiness dimming.
- **Weathering is fully covered by CC0** and is the cheapest way to buy grimdark: `Details for damaged and dirty textures` (rubberduck, 60 GIMP layers of scratches → dirt → damage → blood-splats → cracks → bullet-holes), `~100 grunge brushstrokes and splatters set` (with a `increased_opacity` folder authored "ready for use in UI"), `scrached and rusted metal panel`, and a 2048×2048 tileable stylised metal panel.
- **Industrial gauges and consoles are the weakest area.** Most gauge art found is not CC0 (`Gas gauge` CC-BY, `Vertical Speed Indicator` CC-BY, `Gauge Bar Double` CC-BY-SA, and the whole TRaK2 trim/warning-stripe family is GPL/CC-BY-SA with CGTextures contamination). Surviving CC0: `Alien Jar Hud` (vat/pipe/frame HUD with XCF layers), `Speedometer`, and `Digital Font`'s seven-segment glyph sheet for readout numerals. For full console interiors the existing CC0 answer is still `Sci-Fi interface textures` (rubberduck), already recorded in `ui_hud.md` — not re-listed here.
- **Gothic sci-fi ornamentation is thin but vector.** Two CC0 SVGs (`Dark border`, `Dark shaded buttons` ring ornaments) plus `Medieval Game Button Pack` (CC0, 100 % vector, metal-and-wood buttons) as an ornament donor. The best-looking gothic-adjacent frame art found — `Dark Fantasy UI Pack` — is **CC-BY 4.0**, and is noted in Recommendations as the single justified exception candidate under the `docs/ASSETS.md` exception clause.
- **Kenney contributes nothing grimdark.** Kenney catalogue queries for `dark`, `hud`, `industrial` and `interface` returned no relevant packs; the house style is flat vector by construction. Kenney's role in a grimdark build is only as the *geometry* to darken (already covered in `ui_hud.md`).
- **Openverse is a dead end for UI but usable for texture.** CC0-filtered Openverse queries returned 0 results for sci-fi HUD/interface/cockpit terms; `weathered metal panel` returned only CC0 photographic rust/metal stock (StockSnap). `search_assets` (normalised multi-source) returned nothing licensable for this brief, matching the earlier pass.
- Rejections are dominated by **CC-BY 4.0 dark fantasy UI** (`Dark ARPG UI`, `Dark Fantasy UI Pack`), **CC-BY-SA tactical/military HUD art** (`FPS Military Tactical GUI Icon Pack`, `Racing GUI (Sci-Fi)`, `Golden UI`), and by **two 2026 "cyberpunk" icon packs** that carry a CC0 badge but have had their files pulled by OGA staff for licensing/AI-disclosure problems.

## Recommendations

1. **Fonts: adopt `GGBotNet Fonts CC0 (All-in-1)` (B1) as the grimdark font library, with `Blaec` (B2) as the title face and `Boxy Bold - TrueType Font` (B3) as the readout face.** This is the strongest, cleanest result of the pass and it closes the gap `ui_hud.md` left open ("still no CC0 numerals-specific damage-numbers face") — B3's blocky numerals and B1's 45 faces cover HUD numerals without leaving CC0. Keep `kenvector_future` for body/menu text; blackletter at body size is unreadable and Boxy Bold is too heavy for paragraphs.
2. **Do not hunt for a CC0 grimdark panel family — build one as a treatment.** Take Kenney `UI Pack - Sci-Fi` panels/bar strips (`ui_hud.md` #1, the adoption baseline) as *geometry*, then apply: (a) a dark gunmetal ramp, (b) `C6` scratch/dirt/crack layers, (c) a low-alpha blend of `C8` or `C9` metal, (d) `C7` soot on the edges. Bake the result to PNG and nine-slice it. This keeps one visual language across both the Kenney baseline and the grimdark direction, which is the actual risk of bolting a second art family onto the HUD.
3. **Dark-art sources for windows, inputs and modals: A1 → A2 → A3, in that order.** A1 supplies the widest element set with live PSD layers (`sleek and yet dirty`), A2 is purpose-built dark with real progress bars and input boxes, A3 adds 140 PNGs at 1080p for volume. Normalise outlines and contrast against Kenney before committing any of the three.
4. **Damage-warning stack, in layers:** `C1` (1920×1080 static hull-damage vignette) as the base, `C2` as the sin-driven critical-state pulse (`author's own documented pattern`), `C3` as a short hit-flash, `C4` as an optional splatter decal inside the frame. All four are CC0 and all four are full-screen or decal art, so none of them constrains HUD layout.
5. **Industrial gauges: compose, don't source.** CC0 gauge art is nearly exhausted (`Gas gauge`, `Vertical Speed Indicator` and `Gauge Bar Double` are all non-CC0). Build gauges from `D1` (frame + pipework), `D2` (dial), `B7` (seven-segment numerals), `C5` for state dimming, and the already-recorded `Sci-Fi interface textures` (rubberduck, `ui_hud.md` #9) for console interiors behind the chrome.
6. **Weathering kit: `C6` + `C7` + `C8`,** and gate them behind a single "wear level" so a clean HUD and a wrecked HUD are the same scene with a different layer stack rather than two art sets.
7. **Ornament: `D3` + `D4` + `D5`, all vector, all CC0.** Re-stroke to dark metal. The best-looking gothic frames found in the survey are not CC0 (see the rejected list), so the ornament budget has to be spent on these three plus Kenney `Fantasy UI Borders` (`ui_hud.md` #20, CC0, right nine-slice geometry, wrong ornament) as a geometry template.
8. **One justified exception candidate (do not act on without sign-off): `Dark Fantasy UI Pack - Health bars, inventory containers, buttons`** (https://opengameart.org/content/dark-fantasy-ui-pack-health-bars-inventory-containers-buttons, badge `CC-BY 4.0`) is a 16.7 MB Blender-rendered set of dark, gloomy, horror/doom-tagged bars, containers and buttons — visually the closest thing to grimdark UI found anywhere, and its bars ship **full and empty versions so they can be blended**. It is rejected under the CC0-only rule, but `docs/ASSETS.md` carries an explicit CC-BY exception clause; the required credit string is already documented by the author (`[...] by Of Far Different Nature (https://fardifferent.carrd.co/)`).
9. **itch.io: deliberately not searched.** No page surfaced with a written CC0 statement; `search_assets` returned only `blocked_unclear_license` itch rows, and the OGA "cyberpunk" entries that point at itch bundles are themselves rejected below.
10. **Landing layout (per `docs/ASSETS.md` naming rules).** Fonts stay under `raw/ui/` because no `raw/fonts/` category exists in `docs/ASSETS.md` — this is consistent with `ui_hud.md`, but if the font library grows, a `raw/fonts/` category plus a `font_` filename prefix is the cleaner fix.

### Notes and follow-ups

- **Nothing was downloaded.** Every direct download URL above was read off the asset page itself and is untested.
- **Licence evidence standard:** every candidate row was confirmed by reading the page's `License(s)` line as rendered from `license_images/cc0.png` with the link text `CC0`, plus any attribution notice. Rows marked *(displayed file name; re-check on the page)* had their contents read directly but their download link was only seen in the page's plain-text file list, where the display name can differ from the served filename — the traps already confirmed in this pass (`BloodOverlay.png` → `BloodOverlay_0.png`; `alien_jar_hud.zip` → `alien_jar_hud_1.zip`; `panel.png` → `panel_0.png`; `thing.png` → `thing_0.png`; `Boxy-Bold-Font.zip` → `Boxy-Bold-Font_0.zip`) show why that check matters before spending a download.
- **Share-alike contamination watch.** `D4` (`ring_ornamental_buttons.svg`) is a redraw of Dakal's CC-BY-SA `magic buttons` and the rejected `Gauge Bar Double` / `Blood red health bar` share the same source lineage. If any Dakal-derived art is ever adopted, keep it in its own folder so provenance cannot be confused during `audit_project_assets`.
- **Packs that ship extra formats:** `C6` and `D1` ship layered sources (GIMP XCF) rather than flattened PNGs, so both need a bake step before they are usable as Godot resources. `A1`, `A2`, `A3` and `A7` ship PSDs and will need the same treatment; `C7` and `C8` are large (34.7 MB and 9.5 MB) relative to a HUD need and should be pruned after first inspection.
- **Still open after this pass:** (a) no CC0 heavy condensed slab, military-stencil or "damaged-metal" display TTF exists — `B2`/`B3` are the nearest; (b) no CC0 weathered *bar* art exists at all, so bars must be built from Kenney strips plus `C6`/`C8`; (c) no CC0 dark-metal nine-slice frame family exists, which is the single biggest reason the grimdark direction has to be a treatment rather than a source.
